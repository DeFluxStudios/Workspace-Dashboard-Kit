# Design checklist behind the fire section

A short checklist, kept small to save reading. It is blog-level craft, not research, and it comes from game design
because the same questions apply to wanting to come back to your own work.

## The rules, and where each one is on the page

| Rule | On the page |
|---|---|
| Loop at three time scales, each with a clear objective and reward. Short: under a minute. Mid: about 20 minutes. Long: weeks, with the goal stated early | Spark, Campfire, Beacon |
| Split a big goal into visible milestones, with a marker from the very first step | Rune ring: one rune per project, alight from its first idea |
| Show progress as a share of the goal, so different paths compare fairly | Heat percentage and the next mark, from the `heat` rules |
| Separate marks for different kinds of achievement | Blade, Ledger, Table and Map, each a true count |
| Map rewards to who they serve: those who beat things, complete things, talk to people, or explore | The four marks, one for each. Socializers are the weak spot of solo work; the Friend Space and guestbook are the small answer |
| Vary reward size and timing. Small acknowledgements count | Friends' comments quoting real numbers, the dice, a rare mascot |
| Respond to every action immediately | Sparks on every click, instant rolls and swaps |
| Do not rely on external rewards alone: mastery and recognition count | Quotes from the workspace's own files, not stock slogans |
| Unlock new things so return visits find something new | Webring and Campfire surface forgotten projects. NEW! and HOT! follow what really changed |
| Give people a place to respond | The guestbook |

## The line

**No login pressure, breakable streaks, expiring rewards or losable progress.**

Counts on the page are lifetime only. There are no timers and no "days since". The visitor counter only goes up and is
never used against anyone. Sources on this subject are often written with retention or "addictive" play in mind: take
their craft, not that goal.

## Numbers and evidence

- Every number is counted from real files at build time. A number that is absent or zero is not boasted about.
- A quote must appear in a real file in the workspace, or it is dropped.
- A claim about the owner or their process appears only if their own documents say so.

## Color

Near-black is the neutral, crimson sits at hue 350 and ice blue at hue 200: a split complement, in roughly 60/30/10
proportions. Blue is kept for what is live, clickable or blazing, and is far lighter than the red. The two meet through
black or white, so they do not vibrate against each other. Amber is only for "blocked", which always has a text label.
State is carried by length and dashes as well as color.

## Motion

Motion is on by default in this kit's look, even if the browser reports "reduce motion": some PCs report that only
because a Windows animation setting is off. "Stop the fire" in the page header turns it all off and is
remembered. If the person using a workspace needs reduced motion, set `"motion": "respect-os"` in `dashboard_config.json`.
